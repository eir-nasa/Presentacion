## Capture and publication rules

Use synthetic records and redact real credentials, access and refresh tokens, signing keys, account identifiers, private infrastructure details and sensitive records. Inspect every frame, caption and log before release. State clearly when content is simulated or experimental, and distinguish source-code inspection, team-reported runs and observed verified results. Do not treat hardcoded facial or voice findings as clinical analysis, or screenshots of mock acknowledgements as proof of backend storage.
